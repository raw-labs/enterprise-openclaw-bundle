This is the OpenClaw Enterprise installation kit. It contains the chart,
matched `occ` CLI and site input tools. Images are pulled separately by digest.

- [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md): prepare and install.
- [UPDATE.md](UPDATE.md): back up, check and upgrade.
- [OPERATE.md](OPERATE.md): verify Agents and integrations.
- [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) and [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md): get help.
- [metadata/release.json](metadata/release.json): selected sources and images.

Use `bin/prepare` to verify the package and prepare site inputs. OCE procedures
are under `oce/`. Keep generated files, site settings and credentials outside
the archive.
